Privacy Policy for Realm of RanCraft

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Game URL: https://realmofrancraft.vercel.app/

Welcome to Realm of RanCraft ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you play Realm of RanCraft through our website (https://realmofrancraft.vercel.app/), our Telegram Mini App, or our Android mobile application (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or play Realm of RanCraft, we may collect and store the following categories of information:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign up or log in using Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address, encrypted authentication credentials, and a unique user ID (UID) generated by Firebase.
  • Gameplay & Character Data: Your chosen in-game character name, character customization (gender, hair style, outfit colors), level, rebirth count, inventory items, equipment upgrades, pets, gold, diamonds, quest progress, and PvP/arena activity.
  • Local Device Storage: We store game settings, session tokens, and offline trial save data locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage / IndexedDB) so your progress can be preserved between sessions.
  • Basic Technical & Platform Data: Standard technical details needed to deliver the game securely, such as IP address, browser/device type, and—when launched via Telegram Mini Apps—basic Telegram WebApp session parameters required to display the interface properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to operate, maintain, and improve Realm of RanCraft, including to:

  • Authenticate your login and protect your player account.
  • Save and sync your character progress across devices via cloud storage.
  • Enable multiplayer features such as PvP Arena, leaderboards, and redeem codes.
  • Detect cheating, abuse, or unauthorized access to game services.
  • Fix bugs, monitor server stability, and improve gameplay balance.

3. Third-Party Services

Realm of RanCraft uses trusted third-party infrastructure and services that may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database): Used for user registration, login management, and saving player game data. Learn more at Firebase Privacy & Security.
  • Vercel: Used to host and serve the web application files.
  • Telegram (if accessed via Telegram Mini App): Provides the in-app browser container when you launch the game from our Telegram bot.
  • Advertising Partners (e.g., A-ADS): Our web version may display third-party advertisements. Ad providers may use non-intrusive technical signals to serve and measure ads.

4. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable technical measures—including Firebase security rules and HTTPS encryption—to protect your account and gameplay data against unauthorized access or alteration. We retain your account and game progress for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may log out of your account at any time from the in-game Settings menu, or clear local offline progress by clearing your browser or app data. If you would like to request access to or permanent deletion of your Firebase account and cloud save data, please contact us using the email below and include your registered email address and character name.

6. Children's Privacy

Realm of RanCraft is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so we can delete the account.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new features to Realm of RanCraft. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.

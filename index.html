Realm of RanCraft - 3D MMORPG

Step into a free 3D fantasy world made for your phone. Fight monsters, defeat giant bosses, tame loyal pets and become a legend of the Realm - right in your browser, no download needed.

Game Features

  • 12 Maps - from peaceful Moviex Plains to dangerous boss lands and the Blood Arena.
  • 30+ Skills - summon a giant skeleton, call down anvils and unleash energy waves.
  • World Bosses - Big Boss alerts appear at random, with big Godzilla Fragment loot.
  • Pets - healers, robots, dogs, fairies and birds that follow and help you.
  • Costumes & Diamonds - wings, crowns, masks, swords and armor with special bonuses.
  • Gear & Crafting - random stats, upgrades, rebirth and mounts.
  • AI Players - a lively world with bots, quests and NPC shops.
  • Mobile First - touch joystick, pinch zoom, online account with cloud save (login on any phone).
